## Provenance and limitations

All 13 distinct selected source videos decoded without reported errors and their frame counts match recorded episode lengths. Every selected state was independently recomputed, and a matching-camera render was compared to its original frame (mean absolute pixel errors 1.76–1.88 on a 0–255 scale). All 1,200 episodes have uniquely matched video paths, but full decoding was performed only for selected videos.

Episode and frame indices are zero-based. Saved state index s corresponds to video frame s−1; the final post-step state corresponds to the last frame. Playback time is frame/10, while recorded simulation-state time is s×0.1 s. Images come from original video frames, with external headers and JPEG compression only. Depth is collision-geometry overlap, not a pixel measurement or calibrated material deformation. Camera occlusion can hide the contact surface.

Only the existing two combined JPEG figures are retained for Git. Raw frames, metadata, a frozen hash-verified model, the full audit and scripts are under `archive/current_runs_20261002/`. Previous selected figures and documents are preserved under `archive/previous_before_20261002/`. The archive is Git-ignored; no originals were deleted.
